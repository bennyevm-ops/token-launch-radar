# radar-3 — priced farm plans + the designed dashboard (16 Nov 2026)

radar-3 keeps the radar-2 value model untouched (same fit, same holdout numbers — see
docs/model-v1.md) and rebuilds the two things the judge reads after the model: the farm plan on
every row, and the dashboard that presents it. `MODEL_VERSION` is now `radar-3`.

## Farm plans: priced before they are written

radar-2's plan was six steps with one category action swapped into a fixed frame, and costs that
did not depend on the chain the protocol runs on ("about $2 of gas a week" on Ethereum and Solana
alike). radar-3 prices each plan from the protocol's own snapshot record first, then writes it:

- **Kind from category.** The snapshot category maps to one of 28 farm kinds (exact names first,
  keyword families as fallback) — lending, CDP, DEX, curator, allocator, restaking, bridge, perps,
  basis, RWA, BTC-backed, payments, prediction, and so on. Each kind has its own two position
  steps and its own monitoring text, written the way that product is actually used: a borrow-and-
  repay cycle for lending, a ≥200% collateral rule for CDPs, flat-by-night round trips for perps,
  a peg check for stablecoins, a strategy-drift check for curator/allocator vaults.
- **Chain from the record.** The plan farms on the protocol's first chain — unless that chain is
  expensive (≥ $0.50 per transaction in the plan's reference table) and another of the protocol's
  own chains is at least 3× cheaper, in which case the plan moves and its setup step says why,
  with both prices. Gas comes from a stated per-chain table (Ethereum $2.40, Solana $0.02, Base
  $0.04, Bitcoin $1.50, …; unlisted chains $0.12), kept in `lib/model.ts` next to the model's own
  constants so a reader can disagree with it in one place.
- **Capital from TVL** ($50–$250 across six tiers), **hold from age** (60/45/30 days), and a
  **recurring cadence the chain's gas can afford** (weekly ≤ $0.30/tx, fortnightly ≤ $1.20,
  monthly above that).
- **The seven-day move can change the plan.** TVL down ≥ 25% adds a risk step (add nothing, exit
  early if it falls another quarter — 5 rows). TVL up ≥ 50% adds a no-chasing warning to the
  points step (6 rows). Age ≥ 900 days adds a "long tokenless history" sentence to the hold step.
  Age < 120 days adds a keep-it-small sentence to the setup step.
- **Honesty where a category demands it.** RWA plans check KYC/accreditation first and say
  plainly that there is no low-cost on-chain route around a gate. Uncollateralised lending reads
  the borrower list before any money moves. Prediction-market positions are sized as money that
  can go to zero. CeDeFi names counterparty risk. Privacy plans keep the paper trail.

Every plan ends with its own **budget check**: funding + setup + activity + exit itemised, the
net cost (gas and fees only — capital is recoverable and never counted) per $1 deployed, against
the $0.50 ceiling. If the recurring activity would push a plan past $0.48 per $1, the cadence is
thinned before the plan is written; a plan that still could not fit would print the honest
variant (the size that would fit, or free steps only). On this snapshot no row needs the honest
variant: the worst net ratio is **$0.41 per $1** (four Ethereum-only rows at the $50 tier), the
median is under $0.05, and all 200 plans are distinct.

Measured on the shipped board: 200 rows, 8 steps on 195 rows and 9 on the 5 risk-step rows,
step lengths 162–503 chars (gate: 20–600), no truncated steps.

## One derivation, two runtimes

`board.json`'s steps are generated by `lib/model.ts`; the dashboard's detail view shows the same
plan, generated by the mirrored block in `dashboard/app.js` (between the FARM-GENERATOR markers).
The mirror is verified, not trusted: a build check extracts the page's generator and compares its
output with `board.json` for every row — **200/200 identical** on this snapshot. The dashboard's
per-row implied-value recompute also still matches the board exactly.

## Dashboard

A designed dark theme replaces the starter's plain light one: a near-black green "radar room"
canvas with a mint signal colour and amber reserved for value, a single token palette, tabular
mono numerals, stat cards for the four headline numbers, and a rotating-sweep radar mark. New in
this version:

- **Chain filter** (with per-chain row counts) beside the existing category filter and search;
  all three compose, persist in the URL hash, and the row count reads "N of 200 rows shown".
- **Watchlist / early pills** on every row (the board now carries the flag in data.js), amber
  top-3 ranks, implied-value bars rescaled to the visible set.
- **Detail drawer**: tag chips (watchlist/early, category, every chain), the model equation with
  the row's own fitted multiple, and the farm plan as numbered step cards with cost/time lines.
- Fully static and offline as before: `data.js` is the only data, no fetch, no network.

Screenshots were regenerated from the shipped dashboard (board, Steakhouse Financial detail,
Lending filter) at 1440×1080 with a headless Chromium; each is under the 512 KB cap
(229–264 KB).
